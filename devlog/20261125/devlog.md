**Twenty-five type-ins from 1978 and 1979**

*SoftSide* magazine printed programs for its readers to type in. Here are twenty-five of them from five issues, remade for the browser and gathered on one hub you can sort by name, type, issue or author.

A few to start with:

- **Santa Paravia**: rule a 15th-century city-state, against friends or the computer, and try to be crowned before you die.
- **'Round the Horn**: race clippers from New York to San Francisco round Cape Horn, 1852. The Flying Cloud did it in 89 days.
- **Melt Down**: nine meters, sixteen disasters, and every command helps one thing and hurts another.
- **End Zone**: hot-seat football with secret plays and the clock running.
- **Troll's Gold**: the doors change every turn, and the troll uses them too.
- **Cribbage**, **Pillbox**, **Ten Pin Bowling**, **Spelling Bee**, **Cryptogram** and more.

There are toys too (Storybook, Biorhythms, Kiddy Slot, Comput-A-Sketch) and tools (Calculator, Mortgage, Chess Clock, a ham shack clock and others).

They are prototypes. Tell me which one deserves more.
