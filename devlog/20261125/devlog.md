**Ten type-ins from 1978 and 1979**

*SoftSide* magazine printed programs for its readers to type in. Here are all ten from two issues, remade for the browser.

From August 1979:

- **Melt Down**: nine meters, sixteen disasters, five levels. Every command trades one problem for another.
- **Shooting Gallery**: twenty shots, four rows, one arrow worth a hundred.
- **Cryptogram**: fifty sayings to crack, hints if you need them, a timer if you want one.
- **Morse Code Tutor**: now a metaphor with levels, lives and streaks, plus a keyer and a buddy mode.
- **Storybook**: a toy. Ten questions, then a picture story about the one who answered them.

From October 1978, the very first issue:

- **Pillbox**: an angle, a velocity and a mountain in the way. First to five hits, against the computer or a friend.
- **Gunnery Mate**: fire when a raider is dead centre on a battery. Twenty-one shots, then a rank.
- **State Capital Quiz**: fifty capitals against the clock, with streaks and three apples.
- **Cribbage**: you against the computer, to 121, on a peg board.
- **Calculator**: a tool. A running total and a paper tape.

They are prototypes. Tell me which one deserves more.
