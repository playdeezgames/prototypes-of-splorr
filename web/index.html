SoftSide Prototypes

New takes on the type-in programs from SoftSide magazine, by TheGrumpyGameDev. Code and words made by an AI agent (Claude Code) at the author's direction.

August 1979

  • Melt Down: keep a failing reactor from melting down (after Michael Winter).
  • Shooting Gallery: twenty shots at a carnival gallery (after Frank B. Rowlett, Jr.).
  • Cryptogram: crack a saying in a letter-substitution code (after Rik Pierce).
  • Morse Code Tutor: learn Morse code by ear, level by level (after Donald E. Smith).
  • Storybook (a toy): a picture story starring the child reading it (after Otto Hansen).

October 1978 (the first issue)

  • Pillbox: shell the other pillbox over a mountain; first to five hits (after Gene Perkins).
  • Gunnery Mate: fire when a raider crosses one of yer batteries (after "Death Star").
  • State Capital Quiz: fifty capitals against the clock (after the uncredited quiz).
  • Cribbage: you against the computer, to 121 (after the uncredited listing).
  • Calculator (a tool): a desk calculator with a paper tape (after the uncredited listing).

Keyboard, mouse or touch. Best scores are kept in your browser.
