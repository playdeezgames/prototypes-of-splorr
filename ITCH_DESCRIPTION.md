## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/prototypes-of-splorr/

*SoftSide* magazine printed BASIC programs for its readers to type in by hand. These are new takes on all ten from two issues, rebuilt for the browser. They are prototypes: small, quick, and open to change.

### From August 1979

**Melt Down** (after Michael Winter). A metaphor about running a nuclear plant on the worst day of its life. Nine meters, sixteen disasters, seven commands, and every command helps one thing and hurts another. Bring everything back to 5 before the core reaches 9.

**Shooting Gallery** (after Frank B. Rowlett, Jr.). A metaphor at the carnival: twenty shots at rows of stars, clowns, pipes and ducks, and now and then an arrow worth a hundred.

**Cryptogram** (after Rik Pierce). A metaphor in letter substitution: crack a saying, one letter at a time, or type yer own for a friend.

**Morse Code Tutor** (after Donald E. Smith). The tutor, made into a metaphor: copy characters by ear, two new ones each level, the code gets faster, three lives, and streaks multiply the score. A sending keyer and a buddy mode are there too.

**Storybook** (after Otto Hansen). A toy, not a metaphor: answer ten questions and it tells a picture story starring the child reading it.

### From October 1978 (the first issue)

**Pillbox** (after Gene Perkins). A metaphor in artillery: two pillboxes, one mountain, a barrel angle and a muzzle velocity. First to five hits wins the war. Play the computer or a friend.

**Gunnery Mate** (after "Death Star"). A metaphor at the gun deck: raiders drift over yer batteries, and you fire when one is dead centre. Twenty-one shots, then the Overlord decides yer rank.

**State Capital Quiz**. The quiz, made into a metaphor: fifty capitals, three choices each, a clock, three apples and streaks that multiply the score. A miss comes back later. Or play it the other way round.

**Cribbage**. A metaphor with a peg board: two-handed cribbage against the computer, to 121. Throw to the kitty, peg to 31, count fifteens, pairs, runs, flushes and his nobs.

**Calculator**. A tool, not a metaphor: a desk calculator that carries a running total and prints a paper tape, with the original's one-key functions and its memory.

### How to play

- Open the hub and pick one. Each runs on keyboard, mouse or touch.
- Best scores, records and the calculator's memory are kept in yer browser.
- Found a bug that is not a metaphor? Leave a comment.

### About it

The original listings are in *SoftSide* Vol. 1 No. 1 (October 1978) and Vol. 1 No. 11 (August 1979). These are not ports: the rules and the feel were taken from the listings and rebuilt in Odin compiled to WebAssembly.
